Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Finvestro – The Investment Hub (“Finvestro”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through finvestro.com, why we collect it and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

• Contact form: your name, phone number, email address (optional) and the message you write.
• WhatsApp, phone or email: any details you share when you contact us by these means.

The calculators on this website run in your browser. The numbers you enter in them are not sent to us or stored by us. When you use a WhatsApp button on a calculator, a pre-written message containing your calculator result opens in WhatsApp, and it is sent only if you press send.

2. How we use your information

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, to explain our services (mutual fund distribution, insurance and financial planning) and to keep a record of our conversation. We do not use your details for anything that is unrelated to your enquiry.

3. Who can see your information

We do not sell your personal information. It may be handled by:

• FormSubmit, the service that delivers contact form messages to our email.
• WhatsApp, if you message us there. WhatsApp has its own privacy policy.
• Mutual fund companies, insurers and similar institutions, only if you decide to invest or buy a policy through us, and only to the extent needed to complete your request and meet legal requirements.

We may also share information where the law or a regulator requires us to.

4. Cookies and tracking

At present this website does not use advertising cookies. If we add website analytics in future, we will update this policy.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to meet our legal and record-keeping duties. After that we delete them or keep them in a way that does not identify you.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it or to stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. To do this, write to us at the email below, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no website or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not share passwords, OTPs, card numbers or bank PINs with us through this website or by message.

8. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Third-party links

Our website may link to other websites, such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
