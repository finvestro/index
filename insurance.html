Insurance – Life, Health & General

Protect your family, your health and your belongings.

Insurance protects you and your family from financial loss. Finvestro helps you understand life and term insurance, health insurance and general insurance, in simple words.

Life & term insurance

Gives your family financial support if you are not there. A term plan is pure protection with a high cover at a lower premium. Use our Term Cover calculator to estimate the cover you may need.
Read more about life and term insurance →

Health insurance

Helps pay for hospital stays, day-care procedures and other covered medical costs. We offer health insurance plans from Aditya Birla Health Insurance.
Read more about health insurance →

General insurance

Protects your home, vehicle, trips, personal accident risk and business against loss or damage.
Read more about general insurance →

Where to start?

Many families look at health cover and term cover first, because they protect against two big risks: medical bills and loss of income.

Frequently asked questions

Which insurance companies do you work with?

We offer health insurance plans from Aditya Birla Health Insurance. Please contact us to know about life and general insurance options.

How much cover do I need?

It depends on your family, loans and goals. Our Term Cover calculator gives a starting estimate for life cover, and our health insurance guide explains how to choose a sum insured.

Can I have more than one type of insurance?

Yes. Life, health and general insurance cover different risks, so many people have more than one.
