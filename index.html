Detailed Services

What each service means and how we help

📊

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

A SIP lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds every month, starting from as little as ₹500. It builds a saving habit and spreads your purchases across market ups and downs (rupee cost averaging). We help you pick schemes that suit your risk appetite, time horizon and goals such as retirement or your child’s education.

💰

Lumpsum Investment

Lumpsum means investing a larger amount in one go, for example a bonus, inheritance or sale proceeds. We help you understand equity, debt and hybrid schemes and choose a mix that fits your goals and your comfort with risk.

🔄

STP & SWP

A Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) moves a fixed amount from one scheme to another at regular intervals. A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) gives you a regular payout from your mutual fund investment. We help you set these up according to your cash-flow needs. Tax treatment depends on your situation, so please consult a tax professional.

💡

Financial Planning

We look at your income, expenses, savings and liabilities, then help you set goals with timelines for retirement, children’s education, an emergency fund and more. We review your plan with you from time to time as your life changes.

🛡️

Life & Term Insurance

Life insurance gives your family financial support in your absence. Term insurance is pure protection with a high cover amount at a low premium. We help you estimate the cover you need and compare plans on premium, claim settlement ratio and riders. Try our Term Cover calculator.

🏥

Health Insurance

We offer health insurance plans from Aditya Birla Health Insurance. Health insurance helps with hospitalisation, day-care procedures and critical illness costs. We guide you on individual, family floater, senior citizen and top-up plans, and explain waiting periods, exclusions and the claim process so there are no surprises.

🏠

General Insurance

Protect your assets with home, motor, travel, personal accident and business insurance. We explain the policy terms, coverage limits and claim steps in simple language.

🏢

Real Estate Guidance

We share information on residential and commercial property, REITs and home loan options. Property prices can go up or down, so please verify RERA registration and legal documents before you buy.
