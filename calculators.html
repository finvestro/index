How does the SIP calculator work?

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds every month. This calculator estimates the future value using FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where P is your monthly SIP, i is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. The yearly return you enter is converted to an equivalent monthly rate.

Example: a SIP of ₹1,000 a month for 15 years at an assumed 12% a year means ₹1,80,000 invested, about ₹2,95,931 estimated returns and a total value of about ₹4,75,931. This is only an illustration and not a promise of returns.

Frequently asked questions

What is a SIP calculator?

A free online tool that estimates how much your monthly mutual fund SIP could grow to, from the monthly amount, the expected yearly return and the number of years.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. The calculator assumes a constant return only as an illustration.

What is the minimum amount to start a SIP?

Many schemes allow a SIP from ₹500 a month, and some allow even less. Please check the scheme documents.

What is “value in today’s money”?

It reduces the future value by the inflation rate you choose, so you can see what the amount may be worth in terms of today’s buying power.
