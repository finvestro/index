How does the SIP calculator work?

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds every month. This SIP calculator estimates the future value of your investment using the formula FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where P is your monthly SIP, i is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. The yearly return you enter is converted to an equivalent monthly rate.

Example: a SIP of ₹1,000 per month for 15 years at an assumed 12% a year means ₹1,80,000 invested, about ₹2,95,931 estimated returns and a total value of about ₹4,75,931. This is only an illustration and not a promise of returns.

Frequently asked questions

What is a SIP calculator?

A SIP calculator is a free online tool that estimates how much your monthly mutual fund SIP could grow to. You enter the monthly amount, the expected yearly return and the number of years, and it shows the invested amount, estimated returns and total value.

How is SIP return calculated?

The calculator uses the standard future value formula for a series of monthly investments: FV = P × [((1 + i)^n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i). Here P is the monthly SIP, i is the monthly rate of return and n is the number of months.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. The calculator assumes a constant return only to give an illustration. Actual returns can be higher or lower and are not guaranteed.

What is the minimum amount to start a SIP?

Many mutual fund schemes allow a SIP to start from ₹500 per month, and some allow even less. The minimum depends on the scheme, so please check the scheme documents.

What is the difference between SIP and lumpsum?

In a SIP you invest a fixed amount every month. In a lumpsum you invest one bigger amount at once. You can compare both with the SIP and Lumpsum calculators on this page.

What are STP and SWP?

A Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) moves money from one scheme to another at regular intervals, for example from a debt fund to an equity fund. A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) gives you a regular payout from your investment.
